Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The Hoop Test ("we", "the site") is a daily basketball trivia game. This page explains what data is collected when you play.

Local storage

Your daily progress and stats (hints used, guesses, score, and streak) are saved in your browser's local storage so you can return to a completed puzzle and track your streak over time. This data stays on your device only — it is not sent to us or stored on any server, and it will not carry over if you switch browsers or devices.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on Vercel, which, like any web host, receives standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) when your device loads a page. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser also makes a request to Google's servers to fetch them. We may also use Vercel's privacy-focused web analytics to see aggregate traffic numbers.

Cookies

The site itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your game progress is saved using your browser's local storage, as described above.
