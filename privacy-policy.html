Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The Hoop Test ("we", "the site") is a daily basketball trivia game. This page explains what data is collected when you play.

Local storage

Your daily progress and stats (hints used, guesses, score, and streak) are saved in your browser's local storage so you can return to a completed puzzle and track your streak over time. Your streak, stats, and past results stay on your device and do not carry over if you switch browsers or devices. The one exception is the anonymous score described below.

Anonymous scores

When you finish a daily puzzle, the site sends our server the puzzle number, whether you solved it, how many hints you used, and a random anonymous ID that your browser generates and stores locally. We use this only to count results and show how your score compares with other players. The ID is not linked to your name, email, or any account, and we do not ask for any personal information. Results from archive puzzles are not sent. Our hosting and database providers may keep standard server logs, which can include your IP address.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on Vercel, which, like any web host, receives standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) when your device loads a page. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser also makes a request to Google's servers to fetch them. Anonymous scores are stored in a database hosted by Upstash. We may also use Vercel's privacy-focused web analytics to see aggregate traffic numbers.

Cookies

The site itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your game progress is saved using your browser's local storage, as described above.
