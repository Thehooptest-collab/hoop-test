The Hoop Test ("we", "the site") is a daily basketball trivia game. This page explains what data is collected when you play.

Local storage

Your daily progress and stats (hints used, guesses, score, and streak) are saved in your browser's local storage so you can return to a completed puzzle and track your streak over time. This data stays on your device only — it is not sent to us or stored on any server, and it will not carry over if you switch browsers or devices.
