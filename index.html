How to play

A new NBA player is chosen every day. You have 8 hints, always in the same order: Origin, College Career, Draft, Position, Teams, Accolades, Nickname, Number.

Guess any time — you don't have to reveal every hint. The fewer hints you use, the higher your score:

Score awarded by number of hints used
1 hint100 pts
2 hints90 pts
3 hints80 pts
4 hints70 pts
5 hints60 pts
6 hints45 pts
7 hints30 pts
8 hints15 pts

You also get 3 wrong guesses per puzzle — use them up and the round ends. Come back tomorrow for a new player.
